# Audio sources and credits

All five packaged sound assets are CC0 1.0. Attribution is not legally required by CC0, but sources and creators are credited here for provenance.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (full text in CC0-1.0.txt).

These are modern recordings or sound effects used in a fictional game, not historical KCR archival recordings. See manifest.json for edit logs, download URLs, hashes and limitations.

## steam-whistle.mp3
Hiss of steam train #6 (description: steam locomotive whistle) — Joseph SARDIN
https://bigsoundbank.com/hiss-of-steam-train-6-s3017.html
Real steam locomotive whistle recorded at Martel, France. Despite the English page title, this is listed as a whistle / TRNHorn, not a steam drain hiss.
Changes: Unmodified MP3; runtime playback rate 1.08 and gain adjustment.

## steam-chuff.mp3
single steam train chuff — birdOfTheNorth; original field recording by keithpeter
https://freesound.org/people/birdOfTheNorth/sounds/623817/
A single steam engine chuff extracted and cleaned from a field recording. Upstream original https://freesound.org/people/keithpeter/sounds/125211/ also CC0.
Changes: Public high-quality MP3 preview. Mono downmix, short fades, peak limiting, MP3 re-encode. Repeated by quarter-wheel rotations, runtime pitch and gain change.

## steam-release.mp3
Steam release sounds — Marker #1 — bart
https://opengameart.org/content/steam-release-sounds
Steam hiss sound-effect pack. Recording technique is not documented; this is not claimed to be a real locomotive drain valve.
Changes: Selected Marker #1 WAV. Mono downmix, 100 Hz high-pass, 3.8 kHz low-pass, fades, peak limiting, MP3 encode; runtime slowed to 0.78.

## metal-brake.mp3
train-braking.wav — Radio_fragola_gorizia
https://freesound.org/people/Radio_fragola_gorizia/sounds/400093/
Train braking recorded with Zoom H4n in Gorizia, Italy. Modern field recording used as a metal brake texture, not historical KCR audio.
Changes: Public high-quality MP3 preview. Crop 7–12 s, mono, 600 Hz high-pass, 4 kHz low-pass, fades, peak limiting, MP3 encode. Runtime quiet loop while braking.

## station-crowd.mp3
Chinese speaking crowd — Lenguaverde
https://freesound.org/people/Lenguaverde/sounds/401906/
Mandarin-speaking crowd in Beijing Jingshan Park. Modern ambiance is used at low level for the fictional game; not a Cantonese/Hong Kong or railway archive.
Changes: Public high-quality MP3 preview. Crop 8–32 s, mono, 140 Hz high-pass, 2.2 kHz low-pass, fades, peak limiting, MP3 encode. Played softly only near stations.

## Original synthesis
Rail-joint clicks, a guard hand-whistle cue and fallback sound layers are generated by the original code in audio.mjs. No PA announcements, electric-train door chimes or station voice recordings have been added.

## Verification
Source licenses and bytes were verified; each packaged file decodes with ffmpeg. The audio was not personally auditioned. A Cantonese station-staff shouted recording remains unfilled.
