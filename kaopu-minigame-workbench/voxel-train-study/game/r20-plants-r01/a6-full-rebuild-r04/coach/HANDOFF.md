# Detailed commuter coach R03

API: import `{createCoach}` from `coach/coach.mjs`; call `createCoach(T)` for independent original materials or `createCoach(T, M)` with the full original-matter material API. Result: `{root, setInterior, setDoors, details}`. `setDoors(0..1)` drives both end vestibules and six double-leaf side portals; nonfinite input throws. `setInterior(true)` removes roof and +Y wall, preserving floor/interior/chassis and -Y wall. Default is complete exterior.

Coordinates: metres, X along vehicle/front+, Y left, Z up, rail-head Z=0. Root is body centre. Body ±11.275 m, maximum coupling extent ±11.992 m. Width including steps 3.33 m; roof height about 4.013 m. Bogie centres X=±7.65 m. Six side portals are X=-9.65, 0, +9.65 on both sides, each 1.32 m clear nominal width. Local boarding sill Z=1.245 m. End vestibule doors remain X=±11.08.

Runtime includes all dependencies within R03: coach/retained for original design, lettering and native cast-wheel helpers, src/retained/branding for original contour assets, and parent-provided original-matter for material mothers. No runtime imports outside R03. The source old coach and other project files are untouched.

Implemented: finite open-window/boarding-portal body shell; operable finite sliding door leaves, actual aperture glazing, tracks, rollers, seals, handles and thresholds; 24 compact longitudinal twin-seat modules with original rounded leather and wood material charts, sewn leather edging, support aprons, fixing legs/plates/bolts; six structurally mounted standing poles, overhead rails and 52 suspended loops; seat-end grab rails and finite partitions; flush practical ceiling lighting; original walnut floor/window dressings and crowned roof; four finite watertight smoothly curved upper skirts, raised above bogies and interrupted at door zones. Original company gold contours and layered red/gold wordmark are retained, with original red #a22515 and gold #d6aa61 finishes using the FH88 kernels. No invented English company name.

Rejected sofas, scroll arms, tables, table lamps and pendant globes are absent from active R03. The old rejected salon remains unchanged in its source location. No passenger count is certified from seat-module count.

Validation: `node tests/coach-retention.mjs`. It checks finite geometry attributes, exact fixture comparison for 384 original chassis/end meshes including position/index/attributes/material name (boarding step/rail relocation is explicitly excluded), source-hub reuse, finite watertight skirt edge incidence, all 6 open portals by transverse rays at three heights, and two continuous standing path bands by longitudinal rays at three heights. Geometry is 1,651 meshes after layered brand restoration (see validation.json for actual count). State transitions and nonfinite door rejection also pass. The fixture was generated from unchanged detailed source using Three 0.170.0; remote testing needs only this local fixture and the installed `three` package.

Open scope: source brake topology, pressure/force binding, legacy hydraulic caliper labeling and safety logic are NOT validated by retention. Chassis includes original reservoirs, cylinder, calipers, discs, hanging links, coils, axleboxes and service hoses, but that is not a complete engineering system claim. No traction drive is implied for this unpowered coach. Standing envelope rays are sampled geometric evidence, not an accessibility/evacuation certification. Doors open in an idealized plug/slide motion; anti-trap, actuator/control detail and pressure sealing are not certified. Browser visual review and shaders require the parent's remote QA. Streamline fairings are geometry proposals; no aerodynamic/high-speed certification.
