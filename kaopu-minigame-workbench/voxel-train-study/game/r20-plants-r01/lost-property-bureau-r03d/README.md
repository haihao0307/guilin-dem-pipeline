## Actual reference review and diagnosis
The AMO worksheet photograph and R03c exterior/street screenshots were viewed
side-by-side. The old GPO's visible principles are deep stacked galleries,
aligned outer piers, balcony balustrades, cornice lines, polychrome masonry and
pedimented gables. These inform an authored fictional building, not a survey.
https://www.amo.gov.hk/filemanager/amo/common/form/whatsnew_20140415.pdf
No reference image or private source asset is distributed in this runtime.

R03c's south arcade was at z=-40.5 while the office wall was z=-36.6 and the
roof ended at z=-36.8. Its west roof instead extended over the arcade. This
inconsistent geometry made the side wing read as an attached bare slab below a
large shed roof. The constant-width tower and blank broad pediment made its
crown heavy; the pediment also obscured the top window arches. The chimney was
a constant-radius cylinder. R03d corrects these relationships, not only positions.

## Geometric changes
- Both street wings now have upper open loggias on the same 5.5 m bay rhythm and
  the same outer bearing line as the lower arcade. Office walls remain recessed.
  Open balustrades, layered cornice and gabled attic accents complete the facade.
- A single L-shaped hipped roof envelope is constructed by a union of roof
  volumes minus their inset union. It avoids two unrelated overlapping roof
  sheets at the corner. The public ridge is about 14.5–14.7 m, distinct from the
  lower warehouse roof; drainage/structure engineering is not certified.
- Four occupied tower levels continuously narrow through unequal smooth curved
  setbacks. Half-widths in x/z: [7.1,8.2], [6.35,7.1], [5.05,5.4], [3.8,3.8] m.
  Each shell, window plane, corner pilaster, cornice and floor derives from the
  same profile. The broad blank pediment is removed. A narrow hipped crown and
  small finial reach approximately 28 m; there is no dome. Upper dogleg stairs
  fit inside the narrowed shaft; full accessibility/structural validation is pending.
- The chimney has a substantial plinth, a 2.45 m lower shaft radius tapering to
  1.18 m near the top, a tapered bore with retained wall thickness and a restrained
  open terminal collar. Overall height is approximately 31 m. This is a fictional
  design parameter, not a certified boiler/flue calculation.
