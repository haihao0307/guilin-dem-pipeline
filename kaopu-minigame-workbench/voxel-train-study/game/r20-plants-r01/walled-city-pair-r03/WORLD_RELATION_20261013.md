# City and train world relationship

User-confirmed layout direction, relayed by the coordinating task on 2026-10-13:

- The second large covered train yard is before the walled city.
- The railway curves through the inside of the city at low speed.
- It then passes directly beneath Lion Rock through a mountain tunnel to Sha Tin.
- Low-speed city travel is the main presentation space for the user's “88” and the life on both sides. The low-speed requirement applies both while accelerating away and while slowing for arrival.
- The city is not an independent high-speed passage. This direction does not introduce another unsupported large station inside the city.

This is the agreed fictional game-world relationship, not a claim of an actual surveyed railway route. The current two-building function review continues within its existing scope; this note does not add terrain, railway, station or collision-system implementation to that review.
