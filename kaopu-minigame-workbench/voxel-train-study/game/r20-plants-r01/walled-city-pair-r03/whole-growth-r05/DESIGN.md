No unsupported outward return is allowed above a setback.

## Required geometric consequences

- Ownership boundaries are stable world-space parcel boundaries, not the varying width of a decorative window bay. Front apertures and wall returns follow those same boundaries.
- A changed front moves its wall, aperture, shelter, sill runoff, household addition and drainage coordinates together. The existing nine material mothers remain the source.
- Actual exposed lower roofs become usable terraces only with sufficient depth, a real household entrance and complete exposed-edge guards. Existing ledges without adequate depth do not become fictitious walkways.
- Balcony rights that would be disconnected by a new return are renegotiated explicitly. Drying is moved to a reachable supported platform, with its rods outside the walking strip. New terrace drying has real supported posts rather than multi-metre unsupported wall rods.
- Bridge end landings stay at the actual two floor heights. Short flights have finite treads and risers, supported end landings, continuous side guards, unobstructed door thresholds and nominal below-flight clearance.
- The exact A-F04 1973 household transaction, including its 2.45616 square metre area and adjacent drying agreement, remains unchanged.
- All 27 floor entrance/room/wet-area paths, terrace/drying routes and the changed bridge geometry must be checked at the existing fixed test-body size. This does not create a gameplay navigation or collision system.
