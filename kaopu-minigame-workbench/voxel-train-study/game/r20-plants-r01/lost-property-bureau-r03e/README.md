## Resolved defects
- Old windows were opaque blue-gray slabs, with no joinery or interior trace.
  A shared sashWindow function now generates recessed frames, sash rails,
  fine glazing bars, radial fanlight, stone reveal/sill and 9 mm glazing.
  Glass rays continue into the same scene and count exhausted rays explicitly;
  Fresnel sky reflection and accumulated absorption are bounded approximations,
  not a full multi-bounce spectral glass simulation.
- The roof glazing previously sat over an unbroken slate roof. Real light wells
  now cut the roof, and structural glazing frames connect to roof support.
- Street-facing gables now have backward-extending two-pitch roofs merged into
  the main roof envelope, with overhanging front edges, rather than flat signs.
- Material mother height now contributes to close shading normals; retained
  plaster thickness enters the near-surface field using original macro/micro.
  Brick staggering and mortar union logic are corrected. V26 weather functions
  are actually reachable. Six original mother files remain byte-identical.
- Hall/warehouse views now sit inside completed rooms without the 1.1 m cut.
  Finish floors, wall linings, joined counters, seats, desks, pendants and roof
  trusses share the field scene. Fixture light positions are reused in shading.
  The old cutaway remains a separate layout inspection.

## Visibility diagnosis and finite local expansion
Component images from 04d750 isolated the apparent wall/floor ripples to the
old distance-ratio soft-shadow approximation, not the material albedo or normal.
The corrected point-sun visibility follows real opaque hits, skips empty
candidate bounds and records budget expiry independently. A bound is only a
safe step, never an occluder or an assumption of completed illumination.
The tower's old 150 mm solid plinth was also covering its 33 mm finish floor;
its inner cavity now reaches below the finish. Cut slate tiles retain source
cleavage through explicit per-tile coordinates and subdued surface finishing.
A focused 360 by 240 run (c54cccad) verified these defects with zero primary,
glass or visibility exhaustion. It is not the final quality assessment.

Whole civic and occupied-room bounds now prevent street/sky or unrelated-room
rays from expanding every interior function. Local address/bay candidates and
metric pixel footprints refine only necessary detail; custody state is never
removed by view culling. Full visual regression uses native 960 by 640 frames,
followed by 360 by 240 custody frames from the same commit and browser.
The light model still uses an authored ambient/fixture approximation rather
than full global illumination, and rotated wooden-frame normal coordinates
remain a known binding limitation. These are explicit unfinished quality
boundaries, not reasons to lower the target or call a passing test a finished
artwork.

## Mother-return response diagnostic, after f8d5f9

The rigid-frame correction is frozen at f8d5f94f (Actions 38197817816). Actual 504 GPU material samples were covariant, while all 18 legacy groups failed; six 192×192 ROI frames had unchanged geometry. Beauty moved only 0–2/255, so it was not promoted as a visual upgrade.

This candidate preserves every KpWoodSample field through ReceiverResponse. Only timber materials 4/14/18 opt into the response in explicit QA mode; stone and other materials remain the unchanged visual control. Cavity, original roughness, specularLevel and clearcoat channels are connected using the frozen room-slice GGX response semantics, with the bureau's same sun, ambient, fixtures and display transform. The old mat14 roughness override survives only in baseline mode. ClearcoatRoughness stays at its original value 1; no glossy finish parameter was invented. Macro displacement and parallax fields remain declared, retained and unimplemented rather than secretly changing geometry.

Contribution diagnostics show wood terms after existing glass transmission attenuation, with non-wood/sky masked to zero and without repeatedly adding glass-sky reflection or fog. Their sum is NOT the complete beauty composite. Existing glass remains a forward same-scene continuation plus attenuation and Fresnel sky mix, not full refraction or indirect reflection. This step deliberately adds no lights or environment reflections. It may expose the limitation of uniform indirect lighting rather than supply a large visible improvement; actual same-exposure ROI evidence decides.
