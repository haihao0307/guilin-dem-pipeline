# R10 visual references

These publicly viewable photographs informed original procedural geometry, material choices and small props. The photographs and their pixels are not distributed with this game. This is a selective atmosphere study, not a measured reconstruction of any station or a claim that the game's counterfactual steam service is historical fact.

- [Kowloon station platform, 1958, Andrew Suddaby](https://gwulo.com/media/11761): brick platform face, pale coping, thin dark canopy columns on pale bases, projecting metal canopy and open trusses. The game preserves an unobstructed locomotive/driver view instead of reproducing the complete roof footprint.
- [Tai Po Market station, circa 1970s, Government Records Service](https://www.grs.gov.hk/ws/hip/en/town.html): subdued grey masonry and tiled roof, black-framed benches, bamboo baskets/carrying poles, a small wheeled vendor cart, paper notices and a round clock. The original catalogue image is [711.4095125 MAR 1979](https://www.grs.gov.hk/ws/hip/common/img/record/711.4095125_4.jpg).
- [Tai Po Kau, 1968](https://gwulo.com/media/54042): open hard-surfaced platform, pale coping, light vertical fence, shallow station-name boards and slender lamps. The game uses a sparse furniture combination; a full bay or local landscape reconstruction is outside this pass.
- [Tai Po Market platform, 1958](https://gwulo.com/media/22175): low waiting structures, pale walls and branching column braces. Rural stations are not assumed to have timber platform surfaces.

The small waiting room and tea corner are original gameplay scenery. Their dimensions, contents, lettering layout and placement are artistic choices, not sourced floor plans. The shallow bilingual station boards favor readable Chinese and English rather than duplicating every historic letter orientation. No new people, recorded music, announcements, dispatch choreography or weather logic are introduced by this pass.

All station meshes and procedural textures are authored for this workbench. Existing train, character and licensed audio provenance remains in the respective modules and audio credits.
