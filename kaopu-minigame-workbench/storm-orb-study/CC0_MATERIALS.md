# Material provenance

Created using Rock015 and Moss001 from ambientCG.com, licensed CC0-1.0. Official license permits including raw assets in a video game: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ .

Sources: https://ambientcg.com/a/Rock015 and https://ambientcg.com/a/Moss001 . Exact download URLs, original filenames, SHA256 hashes, byte sizes, resolutions and physical-scale assumptions are in assets/SOURCES.json.

Only necessary original color, OpenGL normal, roughness and rock displacement maps are included. Original reference Storm video/images, unused material alternatives, previews, archives, Blender files and DirectX normal maps are excluded. The PBR textures are source assets; the miniature, shader blending, ground and architecture are independent code, not the original author's Houdini scene.
