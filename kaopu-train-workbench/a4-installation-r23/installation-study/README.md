# Inferred installation family, not historical calibration

The failed baseline stays at middle-cylinder inclination 0.11 rad, rod length 3.5 m and cylinder-axis station 4.75 m. Its measured rod/front-driver axle intersection remains evidence, not deleted or repaired by changing a phase.

The solver tests a coupled family: the cylinder axis passes through the driving crank axis; the crank trajectory and fixed-length rod determine the slide; the piston remains within the chosen cylinder envelope; the slipper overlaps the chosen guide; the middle cylinder is above and behind the outside installation; the rod envelope avoids the leading axle. These relations combine documentary topology with explicitly inferred sizes and design thresholds. There is no measured mounting saddle constraint, because that geometry is missing. “Feasible” means only these sampled local constraints, not an installable or authentic A4.

Grid extent is 0.08–0.20 rad and 3.1–3.9 m, sampled at 1,441 phases. Its 2,620 retained pairs have projected ranges 0.124–0.20 rad and 3.415–3.585 m. These projections do not mean every cross-combination is valid, nor is 0.20 a physical upper bound: it is the search limit. The ranking is an arbitrary declared minimum-change preference, not historical evidence. No unique true installation is identified.

A reversible whole-train diagnostic can use the fixed-length slice L=3.5 m, with 0.126 rad as one labeled hypothetical example. This preserves rod length and eye dimensions and requires a coherent rotation of cylinder, guide, pressure head and slider assembly, not just moving the connecting rod. Saddles, valve alignment, piping, suspension motion, stress, and complete swept collisions remain unresolved. A positive local axle clearance must not be labeled global mechanical acceptance.

The original teacher model has not supplied a measurable inside cylinder/connecting-rod axis. Actual source observations are documented separately, not treated as real-locomotive truth. Real drawing dimensions remain the preferred missing evidence.
