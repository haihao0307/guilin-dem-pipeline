# Research release provenance review

This is not a clean-room or exact 60009 release. Do not remove attribution to manufacture a claim of independence.

- Newly written dynamic coordinate adapter, QA scripts, six crankpin support functions and installation solver are this task's original code. Their sizes and mechanisms remain inferred unless explicitly supported by documentary evidence.
- The 513 preserved analytic fields came from the recovered research Git blob. Original source strings and metadata were retained byte-for-byte. The complete lost generation chain and per-parameter provenance are not recovered, so independence from source-model observations cannot be certified for every field.
- The original anatomical reference is timblewee's “Train Class A4 Union of South Africa”, licensed CC BY 4.0: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/train-class-a4-union-of-south-africa-636ed4890b72429da4a10abec0551373 . Keep this research acknowledgement. No original vertex, index, animation or texture buffers are distributed in the analytic code package.
- Documentary dimensions use The Engineer, 27 September 1935, Silver Link, pages 319–320. Functional relations use official NRM and Sir Nigel Gresley Locomotive Trust maintenance reports. These are different locomotives and do not prove 60009 dimensions. Linked original pages and photographs are not republished as assets.
- The teacher measurement report is a limited source observation. It does not certify hidden absent geometry, nor provide the inside-cylinder inclination, rod length, or true crank phase needed for the current mechanism.

Allowed conclusion: a source-acknowledged whole analytic research diagnostic, preserving inferred/observed/documentary distinctions and failed tests. Not yet supportable: an attribution-free pure-original final replica, complete train mechanics, or a 1:1 60009 claim. No final public release or Pages update has been performed in this stage.
