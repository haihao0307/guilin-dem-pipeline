Research only, incomplete, not an exact 60009 or manufacturing model. Original failed baseline and previous R21–R24 versions are retained. There are still519 analytic fields, unchanged source geometry, no object meshes or point caches, and no coal.

## What now follows Time

Candidate mode uses the same crank theta and same-side crosshead as traction motion. A newly reconstructed finite Walschaerts closure controls both outside valve mechanisms at the retained fixed reversing setting (snapshot label .75). It is not an independent sine wave, not recovery of the lost generator and not historical calibration.

Thirty-six outside mechanism fields and ten moving outside valve-chest fields receive rigid inverse coordinate transforms from that closure. Static trunnions, lift controls, valve guides, shell/liner/end covers/packing and steam stubs stay fixed. Three piston-ring packs follow their respective pistons, as validated in R24. No geometry is stretched to fake rod closure.

The solver enforces the actual retained eccentric-rod/expansion-tail geometry, curved-slot die block, radius rod, lifting link and combination/union-link closure. The common theta drives return crank and crosshead. The reversing control is fixed: no claim of a validated variable cutoff or reversing map. Independent numerical and source endpoint checks are retained under valve-reconstruction/.

## Pressure-body interface

Each outside valve displacement q is spindle-start Z minus4.1. Only retained mPV fields6–10 move: two head cores, two seal bands and shared spindle. The other chest fields stay fixed. The spindle endpoints in chest coordinates are q−.58 and q+.57. This geometrical coupling is not a steam-flow, valve-event, lap/lead, efficiency or torque simulation.

## Explicit control-chain break

The fourteen central/Gresley fields remain frozen. No middle valve chest exists in the retained outside-chest fields. We do not animate the middle valve using the ideal relation −qLeft−qRight or invent its location: even the retained finite snapshot differs from that ideal relation by26.77micrometres. The whole train is therefore NOT mechanically accepted, regardless of outside closure or a positive local middle-rod/axle clearance in the hypothetical installation mode.

The original .11rad middle installation still collides with the leading axle. The .126rad example is only an inferred fixed-length installation candidate, not a unique historical correction. Saddle load paths, full steam/feed network, brakes, cab controls, global swept collisions, true phasing and finished analytic material/surface waves remain incomplete. Diagnostic materials must not conceal mechanics.
