# A4 whole analytic research bench

One bench, two explicit states: preserved failed baseline and inferred whole candidate. This is not a complete or exact60009, a historical reconstruction certificate, manufacturing geometry or a working steam simulation. Previous source versions remain retained.

The candidate uses one Time→rail-local environment→train-object chain. Wheel/traction/piston/ring motion and reconstructed finite outside Walschaerts control feed the finite Gresley linkage. All519 original fields remain unchanged. The central mechanism moves only within its existing inferred horizontal installation: no middle chest is created, no cylinder position is secretly replaced, and no ideal sum is used instead of finite short links.

Current whole-field failure is now explicit: the analytic central levers intersect the analytic main-frame fields, and the analytic middle spindle intersects the middle piston/covers. ## What the checks establish

- Outside finite closure: crank/crosshead inputs are shared with traction; the reversing setting stays at the retained .75 snapshot. New runtime solver, not a recovered lost generator.
- Central finite closure: actual outside spindle outputs feed a2:1 lever, equal lever and three finite short links. Reference endpoints recover, and the finite correction from the ideal sum is retained.
- Rigid field transforms and transformed AABBs preserve field shapes. The source scalar strings and metadata are unchanged.
- The original failed state remains selectable. The candidate's .126rad middle-cylinder inclination is an explicit hypothetical fixed-length installation, not a known real dimension.

## Physical failures remain visible

1. Original .11rad middle rod/front-driver axle collision, measured28.74mm combined envelope penetration. The candidate's local rod/axle clearance does not establish all-part clearance.
2. Original central lever plates share the same axial layer at C. Both retained scalar fields are negative at a common witness and contain a common interior ball of at least6mm radius. Finite pin-center closure does not fix this rod-on-rod interference. Baseline retains this failure. The explicit candidate keeps plate thickness, relocates full plates into derived layers, cuts declared terminal bores and adds eye sections, bushings, spacers and retention envelopes. See stack-reconstruction/ for the 73-field derivation. No verified threaded lock, frame support or middle guide is implied.
3. Middle valve installation/chest, cylinder saddles and fixed-pivot support load paths remain unverified. Complete steam/feed routes, brakes, cab controls, actual crank phasing, valve events, clearances, suspension, stress and finished physical material/surface modelling remain incomplete.

The continuous central certificate is conditional on the two input spindle positions staying inside [4.02,4.18]m. It does not certify the entire upstream outside mechanism continuously or any physical collisions. Numerical residuals are algebraic consistency, not real-world tolerances.
