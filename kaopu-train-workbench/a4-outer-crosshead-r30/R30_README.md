# R30 whole-train outer valve-crosshead candidate

This is one whole-train research bench, not a part demo or an exact 60009 model. R29 and the original failed baseline remain preserved. The original 513 analytic fields plus six earlier attachment fields remain visible in baseline (519); the current candidate has 662 visible fields out of 687 defined.

## Mechanical change

Official SNGLT photos establish an independent forked rear valve crosshead with transverse pin, axial spindle socket and separate open guides. The earlier solid capsule tail and closed box guide are retained only in baseline. Their failures were not motion-mapping errors: 1,441 poses align the original V bore/pin within 1.78e-15 m. Both old tail witnesses and 576 old guide witnesses are preserved for replay.

The candidate adds 32 analytic fields: long rounded twin cheeks and shoes, bearing sleeves, finite pin and retainers, a separate round spindle entering a short socket, a cotter envelope, and open guide supports with separate retaining strips and their own screw holes. No original frame/axle/cover is cut. All movement and material inverse coordinates use the same train Time and existing outside-valve solver. Original lever thickness, pin centre, lever bore, pin shaft and front spindle eye remain unchanged. Existing duplicated spindle aliases are suppressed in candidate so they cannot refill the fork.

The retained installation leaves only 55.395 mm between maximum-travel V and the rear cover. The candidate's forward extent is 53 mm, with 8.5 mm socket engagement. Those are explicit GAME fitting parameters, not measured original dimensions or a strength claim. Guide roots share a declared fixed interface with the retained cover face; whether the prototype root was integral is not proved by a photograph. New strips/bolts have finite mating holes, but threads, cotter taper, spacing button and exact historical locking remain unresolved.

The first new pin-head/washer-to-guide-cap collision is recorded, not hidden. The whole shoe/rail/5 mm cap stack was lowered 3 mm, retaining its thickness and internal connection, to obtain a 1 mm declared clearance. Original shaft centres and cover position were not moved.

## Evidence and tests

See OUTER_CROSSHEAD_GUIDE_EVIDENCE.zh-CN.md for exact official URLs and image-specific visibility limits. No official photographs or restricted models are included. Photos concern 60007; this is not a claim to 60009 dimensions. Existing analytic-source attribution and incomplete generation provenance remain in PROVENANCE_REVIEW.md.

CPU tests check source/baseline preservation, 1,441 same-Time poses, independent emitted-GLSL expression evaluation, material advection, shaft-front preservation and finite boundaries. Independent whole-neighbor searches are scoped searches, not a continuous global clearance certificate: 72 phases formed the AABB broadphase, then 68 pairs were searched at one phase and 48 pairs at six selected phases. Of 116 relationships, six negative contacts belong to explicitly checked same-rigid-body connections; 110 no-witness outcomes remain unresolved, not certified clear. The R29_BROWSER_QA_HISTORICAL.json records the prior verified browser; it does not certify R30.

## Native sampling

Default 720x400 retains the exact prior shader text. The optional resolution=1440x800 page parameter changes native pixel coordinates, viewport, scissor and sampling footprint only. No mesh, textures, image upscaling, new mechanical clock or shape displacement is added. The old renderer snapshot is tested against the same R30 mechanics; R29 source snapshots are separately preserved. Actual native PNG dimensions and same-driver 720 equivalence must pass the new browser run before being claimed.

## Still incomplete

Whole mechanical acceptance remains false. Baseline rod/axle interference is intentionally still a failed test. Conditional middle frame-slot bounds do not certify all-phase input containment. Current middle installation dimensions, global continuous clearance, complete steam/feed/exhaust network, braking, cab controls, suspension and final casing remain incomplete. Local connector corrections and rendered motion are not a finished locomotive.
