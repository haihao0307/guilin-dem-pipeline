# A4 middle-valve cover: photographic topology evidence

Verified 2026-10-25 from public SNGLT official reports, using an independent cloud-browser tab. Read-only research; no model or repository changes.

## Result

The installed LEADING middle valve cover is externally flanged and fastened around its perimeter. Replacing it with a naked recessed end-plug would contradict the strongest primary photograph found. Its exact contour, stand-off, fastener coordinates, and dimensional envelope are NOT established by a calibrated drawing here.

## Safe modeling conclusion

Keep separate identities for cylinder cover, leading middle valve cover, gland, crosshead, guides, trailing middle valve cover, tailrod cover and oil fittings. External perimeter fastening at the leading middle cover is supported directly. Exact dimensions and fastener pattern remain unresolved. This finding authorizes no geometry changes by itself; it removes the proposed recessed-plug interpretation from the evidence-based options.

Images were downloaded only for local inspection/reference. Public accessibility is not a redistribution license; do not publish these photographs as workbench assets without rights.
