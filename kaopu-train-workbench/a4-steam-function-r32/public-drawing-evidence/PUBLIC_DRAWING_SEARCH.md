# A4 public drawing search: bounded result

Checked 2026-10-25 UTC. Read-only public-source research; no geometry, source model, Git, account, payment, or publication changes.

## Outcome

**No readable K438/K413 installation drawing, and no independent middle-valve/cylinder installation dimension or relative datum, was obtained.** This is a bounded negative search result, not proof that no public scan exists. Current installation numbers remain unverified. In particular, this search supplies no justification for Y = 1.48 m or for moving, tilting, cutting, or deleting geometry to remove interference.

## Genuine public primary scan acquired and visually checked

The Engineer, 27 September 1935, pages 318–320, article beginning “L. and N.E.R. — The Silver Jubilee Train”. The article explicitly attributes supplied design information to the railway. Publisher-hosted public scan:

https://theengineer.markallengroup.com/production/2018/09/Gresley-Silver-Jubilee.pdf

Also verified public mirror path on the same publisher host:

https://theengineer.markallengroup.com/production/content/uploads/2018/09/Gresley-Silver-Jubilee.pdf

All three scanned pages were rendered and viewed. Page 319 has an exterior photograph and descriptive text; page 320 has an external side/front weight-and-dimension diagram plus principal dimensions. It confirms three separately cast cylinders and outside Walschaerts / inside equal-and-2:1 Gresley actuation. **The plate does not expose or dimension the middle valve/cylinder relative installation.** No scaling, slope extraction, or inferred hidden axis was performed. Local PDF and p. 320 render are retained for private evidence review only; public readability is not a republication licence.

Files: `engineer-19350927-silver-jubilee.pdf`, `engineer-19350927-silver-jubilee.txt`, `engineer-19350927-p320.png`.

## Catalog and collection checks: identity only

Current NRM public catalog, dated 23 March 2020:

https://www.railwaymuseum.org.uk/sites/default/files/2020-03/Doncaster%20Locomotive%20Drawings%20and%20Microfilm%20Lists%2003%202020.pdf

Indexed catalog entry for K438 appears on printed page 185: arrangement of valve gear, three-cylinder engines, 20 June 1938, A4, supersedes K413; two copies, plus dyeline tracing. This is a list, not the drawing, and adds no dimensions to the earlier review.

Exact K438/K413 and A4-drawing searches across Science Museum Group collection/archive domains returned no usable drawing image. Opening the archive root returned HTTP 403, and the collection K438 query was inaccessible through the web tool. No login or alternate authenticated access was attempted. Those failures limit coverage; they do not prove absence from the archive.

Official A4 preservation trust describes the NRM as the route for ex-LNER/BR drawing copies and states it cannot supply those drawings itself:

https://www.sirnigelgresley.org.uk/education.shtml

No contact, copy order, or purchase was made. The already-known Q104 museum-shop preview was not treated as a replacement for K438.

## Rejected near match

Engineering, 17 December 1937, pp. 682–683, public scan:

https://graces-guide-s3-live.s3.amazonaws.com/gracesguide/1/19/Engineering-19371217.pdf

The search hit discusses Gresley actuation but the actual article concerns reconstruction of locomotive No. 10,000 (W1), not A4. Its dimensions were explicitly rejected as A4 installation evidence. The scan was read locally after the web parser reported its 27.5 MB size was too large. No A4 geometry was derived from it.

## Exact gap and next evidence threshold

Needed: readable complete K438 (20 June 1938) and any applicable later amendments, with title/date, scale, dimension strings and sections showing the middle valve axis relative to its power-cylinder axis, front valve guide/mounting face, frame/rail/axle datum and conjugated-lever support. If K438 omits cylinder/valve-chest placement, its referenced cylinder or general-arrangement sheet is also needed. Confirm the target 60009 era before treating a class drawing as its exact configuration.

Until such evidence exists: preserve the frozen geometry; keep frame-slot architecture evidence separate from unresolved material intrusion into the middle cylinder, piston and covers. The current arrangement cannot be certified as a real A4 installation from the evidence acquired here.
