## Positive evidence actually viewed

Official Sir Nigel Gresley Locomotive Trust overhaul report, 23 December 2016, photograph credited to Darrin Crone:
https://www.sirnigelgresley.org.uk/overhaul-1522/overhaul-04.shtml

Original image link read from the displayed lightbox DOM:
https://www.sirnigelgresley.org.uk/overhaul-1522/pics/o240.jpg

The enlarged photograph was visually inspected, not merely its caption. It shows the front face of the middle-cylinder casting: a large power-cylinder bore and a separate smaller valve-chest bore beside it on the image's right, both with their own flange/stud arrangements. The official caption identifies the smaller bore as the middle valve chest, with its liners removed. The photograph therefore supplies independent installation topology: the middle valve does not share the power-cylinder bore axis. The visible sideways separation is a strong warning against the model's unproved centre-plane, horizontal middle-spindle installation. It is not a numerical coordinate measurement.

## What this changes, and what it does not

The correct next installation model must account for this distinct bore/axis and its guide/linkage connection, rather than merely adjust the elevation of the current spindle until the power-cylinder interference disappears. This evidence materially narrows the root-cause diagnosis, but does not resolve the complete installation transform.

Do not infer a signed train-transverse X offset, rail-relative Y height, longitudinal Z station, bore angle, or numerical distance from this oblique photograph. Image-right is not a verified train-coordinate sign. No scaling from presumed cylinder diameter was attempted. The photograph has no ruler, calibrated camera, or rail/frame datum. It concerns preserved 60007 in 2016, not an authenticated drawing of 60009 in a chosen era. The stronger shorthand claim that this photo alone mathematically disproves every possible X=0 axis would exceed the evidence: orientation and projection are not calibrated.

The original and candidate mechanical-failure witnesses remain valid for the current model. No geometry was altered and no new mechanical pass is claimed.
