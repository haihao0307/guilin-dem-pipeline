# Landscape Mother B3.1: volumetric sample

The public entry opens a colored, rotatable 3D sample directly. No file-selection or source-fitting workflow is required. The B1 fitted-surface tools and B2 experiments are not imported at runtime. This implementation is based on the visible features of the user's latest three photographs; `reference-basis.json` distinguishes observations from authored dimensions, hidden geometry, depth, material values and procedural choices. No geologic simulation, measured photogrammetric reconstruction, family recovery or AAA art approval is claimed.

The main rock, crown/ledge soil and angular fragments are closed numerical solids. Fixed-grid isosurface geometry has actual front, back, sides, lower surface and cavity interiors. The supporting ground is an explicitly identified display/placement surface, not a source DEM. Rock, soil and rubble topology gates count boundary edges, nonmanifold incidence, degeneracy and signed volume. Small disconnected isosurface artifacts in the authored main body are removed once during construction and counted in the receipt. This cleanup never runs on original data or during camera motion.

Ground contacts are checked against the final ground triangle planes. Habitat root positions are intersected with final soil triangles. Vegetation is not faked with painted green cover or placeholder trees; the rooted numeric interface is exported only when requested. Real vegetation remains a separate Mother integration. Fragment material coordinates rotate with each fragment.

No images, external models, numeric textures or texture samplers enter the sample. Scalar/vertex occlusion and fixed-light visibility are precomputed from numeric fields; material microstructure is evaluated arithmetically. The lighting direction is fixed to match those visibility attributes. Exposure may change, geometry and material detail do not change with distance or device. Geometry is built in a worker and drawing uses one bounded GPU submission at a time.

`node test.cjs EVIDENCE_DIRECTORY` runs eleven grouped invariant checks, including deterministic repeat, complete buffers, closed main/soil/talus solids, real ground contacts, soil-root support and prohibited texture/LOD paths. These tests do not establish artistic quality or physical simulation. `python stage.py --output SITE --commit SHA` produces a self-contained HTML entry. `browser_qa.py` runs the actual sample in Chromium and inspects front, side, back, crown, base and underside at a 2048 x 1152 viewport and a mobile viewport. Software CI rendering is not end-user hardware performance evidence.

Local browser navigation was blocked by the environment administrator. Local GLES/EGL images were used only for internal geometry/shader review and are not labeled browser execution. Actual staged and public browser results come from the workflow. Screenshots are audit evidence only and must never substitute for the online interactive result.
