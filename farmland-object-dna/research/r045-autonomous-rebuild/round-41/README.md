# Farmland Mother R045.41 — constrained adjacent-row shoulder continuation

R045.40 passed the real-browser path and all safety/inheritance gates, but its final numeric summary still failed its own materiality target: only 5 weak samples gained materially and only 5 crossed the active threshold, while the gate required more than 8 material gains and at least 6 crossings. A diagnostic sweep then showed the deeper issue: inside the sampled safe weak domain, R40's original junction eligibility itself produced only those five material candidates. The gate/eligibility pairing was therefore internally impossible; merely lowering the gate would hide that contradiction rather than improve the terrain system.

R045.41 keeps the original materiality target and does not raise risers. Every already-active R40 terrace sample, stair step, phase, raw response and the inherited 0.84 amplitude remain exact. R40's junction class is retained, and one narrowly defined class is added: an R39 weak shoulder with nonzero prior support (`mask >= .02`) may be promoted only when it lies directly beside a continuous three-sample (`-6/0/+6 m`) active ribbon on one adjacent contour row, with every support sample in the same terrace family and stair-compatible. New R41 cells never support further R41 growth. Agricultural-slope, family-envelope, drainage and foreground-receiver safety gates are reapplied, and the <=12 m drainage core remains a hard zero zone.

Logic correction: a successful browser render is not evidence that terrace topology is correct, and a low fragmentation ratio is not proof that a branch/merge is agricultural truth. Likewise, an impossible numeric target cannot be repaired honestly by weakening its threshold. This round changes the eligibility model in one explicit, auditable way and then retains the old total material/topology gates.

Evidence boundary: this remains synthetic morphology. The 6 m QA lattice, three-sample shoulder rule, 12 m drainage hard core and generated junctions are not surveyed Yunnan engineering dimensions or measured terrace branch/merge locations. Current macro DEM and photographs cannot supply field microtopography, parcel/management boundaries, bund/riser/channel sections, inlet/outlet sill elevations, hydraulic connectivity or event-level water-management state.
