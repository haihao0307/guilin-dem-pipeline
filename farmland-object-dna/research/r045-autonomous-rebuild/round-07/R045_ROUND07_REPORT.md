## Logic corrections before implementation

1. **Adding surface noise does not solve a long designed foothill bench.** Noise changes texture, not the synchronized lateral position of the slope-to-plain break. R07 therefore changes the *phase* of that transition across x, with drainage-aware damping, instead of adding random roughness.
2. **A contour line is not a rice terrace.** A usable bench terrace also needs a bench surface, riser geometry, cut/fill stability, bund/freeboard logic, an outlet/drainage relation and locally valid dimensions. The first R07 pilot is therefore explicitly `preview-only`; its dimensions are synthetic test values, not Yunnan survey truth.
3. **Passing browser startup is not equivalent to a publishable workbench.** This round finally obtained a deterministic browser screenshot and ready-DOM marker in GitHub Actions, but the audited page is an internal fixed-view diagnostic, not the final public workbench.
4. **Numerical gates do not override the image comparison.** The pilot follows level contours very accurately and avoids drainage, yet it still looks too narrow, too parallel and too regular compared with the supplied terrace reference. Visual acceptance remains false.
5. **Generic terrace standards cannot be promoted to local dimensions.** NRCS and FAO guidance supports contour-following layouts, stable outlets and the need to design bench/riser relations, but it does not supply local Yunnan bench width, riser height, bund size, soil strength, infiltration or irrigation truth.

## Constraints that still prevent a real-world terrace design

The current project still lacks field-survey values for local bench width distribution, riser height/slope, bund crest/freeboard, channel invert/outlet elevation, soil erodibility/shear strength, infiltration/percolation, ponding depth, event flow and maintenance history. Therefore R07 may validate geometric relationships only. It must not claim hydraulic performance, stability, construction quantities or local historical authenticity.

- FAO terrace guidance: bench terrace dimensions and grades depend on site conditions; examples are generic, and outlet/drainage protection is part of the design rather than an optional visual detail.

## Implemented changes

### A. Drainage-damped lateral phase staggering of the foothill transition

R06 still read as a long synchronized toe/bench. R07 introduces an x-dependent z-offset only across the foothill/lower-slope band:

- low-frequency lateral phase variation instead of high-frequency noise;
- damping near inherited drainage carriers so stream positions are not casually displaced;
- zero effect on the rear mountain/upper slope and on the front receiver centerline;
- an explicit fade into the lower plain.

The first R07 trial extended the warp too far and generated a local curvature problem. It was revised to reduce amplitude and change the fade. The current output still has one unresolved local derivative gate, so this change is *not* declared finished.

### B. First bounded contour-following terrace pilot

A single high-permission patch is selected deterministically. Three nearby near-level contour traces are generated by tangent integration and iterative projection back to the target elevation. They terminate when terrace permission falls, when the edge is reached or when drainage clearance becomes too small.

Current synthetic preview parameters:

- seed: `x=5, z=-60`, elevation `12.298529 m`;
- seed terrace permission: `1.0`;

These dimensions are intentionally labelled synthetic. They are not a local standard and are not propagated across the slope.

## Fixed-view visual review

The screenshot was opened and reviewed after the browser run.

### Real improvement

- The previous idea of a single perfectly synchronized horizontal foothill break is less dominant. The lower transition now varies laterally rather than reading as one mathematically identical z-band across the whole width.
- The pilot is genuinely attached to the terrain: all three strips curve with the local contour rather than being drawn as straight horizontal ribbons.
- The pilot stays away from the major drainage carrier in the close view.

### Visual rejection

**Visual acceptance remains false.** The screenshot exposes four important defects:

1. The broad middle agricultural slope is still much too smooth and empty relative to the supplied terrace landscape reference.
2. The visible drainage skeleton still reads too much like a set of drawn/engineered lines. The channels are not yet expressed strongly enough through surrounding valley shoulders, local convergence and terrain incision.
3. The terrace pilot is too small, too uniformly narrow and too parallel. The supplied reference shows wider management-scale shelves, significant width/length variation and nested irregular boundaries.
4. The current whole-site audit camera does not present the front receiver river strongly enough to judge the entire `rear ridge -> slope -> foothill -> plain -> river` chain in one image. The browser check passes, but the visual evidence framing itself needs improvement.

The pilot therefore proves only that bounded contour-following bench geometry can be generated and browser-audited. It does **not** yet prove a credible rice-terrace morphology.
