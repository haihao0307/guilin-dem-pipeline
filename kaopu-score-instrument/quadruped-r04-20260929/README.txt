KAOPU Quadruped Instrument R04

KAOPU_QUADRUPED_K4.js is the pure instrument. It contains no animal scores, player UI, camera or identity preset.
KAOPU_QUADRUPED_PLAYER.html is an empty offline player. Without a K4 score it produces no animal.
The three .score files are separate data.

The K4 score owns body volumes, front and hind limb chains, optional tail, symmetric/central details, materials and optional cover operators. The instrument only parses, validates and calculates the continuous 3D result.
