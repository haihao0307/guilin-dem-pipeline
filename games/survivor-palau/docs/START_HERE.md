## Evidence boundary

Palau's Rock Islands are a valid reference for a forested coralline-limestone / karst seascape. UNESCO describes hundreds of forested limestone islands, coral reefs, marine lakes, caves, arches, coves and characteristic mushroom-like forms. The Airai Complex has sheltered Rock-Island reef environments.

The exact game island, 1944 vegetation layout, patrol routes, canoe type, and rescue event are not claimed as site-specific historical reconstructions yet.

Banana is treated as a **cultivated-remnant candidate**, not as dominant wild Rock-Island vegetation. Palau-wide agricultural sources support banana as an important crop. Banyan/fig is plausible at the Palau flora level (Ficus records exist), but the exact species and exact island placement remain to be verified.
