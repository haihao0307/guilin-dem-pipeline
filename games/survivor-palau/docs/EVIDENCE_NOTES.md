# Survivor: Palau — environmental evidence notes

## Strong enough for world-shape direction

- UNESCO Rock Islands Southern Lagoon: numerous large and small forested limestone islands in a lagoon protected by a barrier reef; the site includes 445 coralline limestone islands, coral reefs, caves, arches, coves and marine lakes. Many islands have mushroom-like forms.
- Palau Rock-Island marine-environment literature describes sea-level notches and undercut limestone bases, with dense vegetation often descending close to the water.
- The Airai Complex of Rock Islands is documented as a sheltered Rock-Island reef setting.

## Strong enough for broad vegetation candidates, not exact site placement

- Palau-wide agriculture sources list banana, taro, cassava, coconut, breadfruit and other crops. This supports banana as a human-use/cultivation candidate, not automatic wild-island coverage.
- USDA Micronesian tree-name records include multiple Ficus/banyan records for Palau. This supports a banyan/fig visual family as plausible; species-level and site-level placement still needs checking.
