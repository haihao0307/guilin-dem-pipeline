# R03 action-comparison stage

This entry runs 18 self-authored paired motion programmes across the original 36 native characters. It provides same-program comparison, programme/role rotation, 1.14× / 1× / 0.55× timing and foot-contact IK. Every displayed character retains the full original mesh and skinning influences. The original R01 and equipment R02 entries remain available.

The new GlovesR03 changes only the cuff-mouth profile and connecting lip. The earlier cuff audit omitted part of the forearm at the mouth; stricter full-CSR testing exposed existing small forearm protrusions on four rounded-female forms. The R03 fix does not hide skin, alter body parameters or replace the full characters.

## Surface-contact work remains separate

The previous coarse sphere/capsule experiment was not sufficiently close to the skin. This action-stage entry therefore does not instantiate that collision engine, show proxy hits as skin contact, or run body-hit responses. Paired blocks, slips, ducks and counters in the 18-program library are authored choreography.

A separate opening/body-counter diagnostic was numerically explored, but its coarse-proxy contacts do not establish a skin-surface hit. It also has a separate extreme-wrist fitting limitation. It is withheld from this entry pending precise deformed-surface and actual-glove contact validation. No artificial hit events or disabled guard targets are used to bypass that requirement.

## Measurement scope

The original 18-program numerical measurements cover the authored base poses and original 36 rests, with contact feedback disabled. They are distinct from browser rendering, glove enclosure and future contact-system review. Full-CSR sole measurements include a sub-millimetre minimum of about -0.265 mm; foot-bone locking is not represented as exact zero skin penetration.

All programmes are self-authored. No teacher neural network inference or teacher-generated boxing clips run in this entry. CI SwiftShader and offline fixed-step videos are not hardware frame-rate or physical-phone tests.
