# R02 Stage 1: ring and structured gloves

This stage changes equipment and viewing only. The accepted R01 36 characters, 18 pairs, full canonical topology, native state recipes, skin/clothing, choreography, timing and contact IK remain unchanged. R01 remains at ../../boxing.html. R02 does not run any of the five research neural models.

## Ring sources and limits

- World Boxing, *Competition Rules, in force November 2024*, §13.12: https://worldboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/World-Boxing-Competition-Rules-Nov-2024-Approved.pdf
- Ringside official current folding-ring product and actual photograph: https://www.ringside.com/ringside-folding-collapsible-boxing-ring and https://www.ringside.com/media/wysiwyg/FOLDING_16X16BOXING.RING.png

The rule reference supplies 6.10 m clear width between ropes, minimum 7.40 m platform, 1.00 m deck, 1.5–3.0 cm padding, four nominal rope levels (0.40/0.70/1.00/1.30 m), four protected corners and two keeper straps per side. This authored scene uses those proportions, 2.5 cm pad and 5 cm covered rope diameter. The maker photograph was actually inspected for ropes, spacer straps, covered attachment points and corner structure. Its floor-ring construction is not misrepresented as the same 1 m platform model.

Only original parametric geometry and original canvas text are included. No maker photo, logo, pattern, scan or downloaded texture is republished. This is a proportion/structure study, not a brand replica, a complete event venue, safety certification, engineering instructions or a dynamic rope/collision simulation. Required real-event accessories and officiating/medical systems are outside this visual stage.

The full ropes are on by default. Transparent/hidden modes are explicit observation controls, not hidden performance reductions. No person or bone update is dropped in any mode. Equipment geometries are shared across all 18 rings; motion still updates all 36 complete characters. Real ring proportions make people smaller in the 18-ring overview; use whole-ring and pair/glove close views.
