## Biological evidence and its limits

Goldstein & Katowitz, The male eyebrow: a topographic anatomic analysis, 2005, PMID16052142, DOI10.1097/01.iop.0000169253.68675.18. Prospective observations of 222 male participants located the brow relative to pupil, lateral canthus and supraorbital rim. Reported mean inferior/superior boundaries above the pupil differ by about8.65mm; eye depth affects brow position. These are cohort observations, NOT a universal root-band thickness and NOT a female/all-age prescription. Primary abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16052142/ .

Dagi Glass et al., The lateral brow: position in relation to age, gender, and ethnicity, 2014, PMID25069068, DOI10.1097/IOP.0000000000000095. Cross-sectional observations excluded significant plucking/tattooing and measured lateral tail location. Individual and group differences make a single aesthetic outline inappropriate as biological truth. Primary article: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25069068/ .

Gupta et al., The Science and Art of Eyebrow Transplantation by Follicular Unit Extraction, 2017, clinical technique/case-based report, PMCIDPMC5561712. The authors distinguish medial head, wider/dense body, and tapering/lower-density tail. They describe different hair directions across these regions. Use this as practitioner anatomical description, not as a population-wide follicle map; surgical ideal-placement rules are not applied as universal limits. Primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5561712/ .

Two independent surface-bound regions follow the retained brow landmarks, keep the interbrow gap, have rounded medial caps, broader body and a progressively narrower tail. Upper and lower bounds are explicit. Regional proportions0–.15/.15–.75/.75–1, half-width eye-span scaling .135 clamped to3.5–4.7mm, and submillimetre endpoint extensions are transparent authored candidate choices, not literature measurements. Different-person shape controls are intentionally deferred until user domain approval.

Colored masks and boundary lines bind to exact skin triangle barycentrics. They are inspection overlays, not physical tissues or permanent skin edits. No eyebrow fibers are generated in this stage. Default view is the actual domain, not a hair beauty image. Depth testing stays enabled and the original skin/eyes/lighting remain intact.

Lash arc length follows the user-requested center-long envelope .22+.78*sin(pi*u)^1.8. Small stable variation and shorter juvenile hairs remain. Curves are normalized by actual sampled arc length so lateral fan or curling cannot erase the length profile. This is an authored naturalistic design, not a claimed measured mean human eyelash distribution.
