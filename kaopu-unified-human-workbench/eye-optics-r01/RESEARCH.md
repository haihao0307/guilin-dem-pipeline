# E1-R: native iris tissue study, not a finished film eye

## Decision and scope

Keep the ET13 native face, eye support, upper/lower eyelids, identity controls and one viewer. Reproduce the owned ET08 iris field first, then test a small material-only tissue upgrade on exactly the same face/camera/lights. Do not import a replacement head/eyeball, create another workbench, or merge/deploy this candidate.

This increment is a 3D native-material experiment. Iris detail is a height field affecting surface normals, not resolved 3D crypt walls or geometry silhouette. The outer wet normal is separated from the tissue normal. Film-quality completion is explicitly **not** claimed.

## Actual source comparison

1. ET13 fixed base `2702e9482cfd1655411bb0920929872669301334`: `face-transfer/FaceSkin.mjs`, blob `d24a7d3a1d575782b7b7dd105df55d3bdd734e66`, defines `fEyeColor`. Its visible iris is two sinusoidal terms, a constant brown base, a pupil radius fixed at 0.43 of the iris mapping, and a dark outer ring. No iris texture, relief, view-dependent refraction or eye-specific optical stack appears in this function. CommonSkinLayer has one shared material and a zero skin-coverage mask on eyes; its original clearcoat is effectively zero there.
2. `FaceFields.mjs`, blob `b3c80676654af60e4169851ffec86b1e324e073a`, derives each iris center/radius from GNM component/material IDs. Its `fEye` is normalized XY, a front mask, and a side index. Preserve it. No pasted ET08 socket coordinates are valid in this frame.
3. ET08 fixed source `bbd6de3ed7904429c7720e74c2297d1f04862314`: `eyes/EyeSystem.js`, blob `134f82d8f26b2ab46fdd7cb32fefe4e3e828175f`, already has an owned deterministic 1024×256 iris field, crypt channel, sclera vessels, pupil motion, wet layer and a one-interface refracted lookup. The source and its REFERENCE_STUDY explicitly say these are independently implemented and not copied XG meshes/textures. This candidate reproduces that exact iris field, verified byte-for-byte against the fetched source before modification. See ET08-REPRODUCTION.json.
4. ET08 `ResearchEyes.js` modifies a fitted globe into a corneal cap and binds contact to scan-specific eyelid rails. It is useful evidence but its SOCKETS, eye axis, radius, scan fit and lid system cannot be dropped into GNM. The old eye shader treats the iris as a plane at 0.83 globe radii and uses one `1/1.376` refraction. That omits the back corneal boundary and aqueous interface.

## Primary teachers and use decisions

- [Disney / ETH, High-Quality Capture of Eyes, 2014](https://studios.disneyresearch.com/2014/11/19/high-quality-capture-of-eyes/): strong teacher for individually varying sclera/cornea/iris surfaces and non-rigid pupil-linked iris shape. It argues against a uniform colored ball. Capture optimization, measured per-person geometry and the dataset are not reproduced here. The website retains copyright and disallows reposting the work without permission; no paper images or dataset are shipped.
- [Disney / ETH, Lightweight Eye Capture Using a Parametric Model, 2016](https://studios.disneyresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/Lightweight-Eye-Capture-Using-a-Parametric-Model.pdf): useful for radial material-coordinate trajectories, pupil-dependent deformation and separate sclera appearance. The implementation here is an original controlled field, not its learned prior or captured iris database.
- [François et al., Image-based modeling of the human eye, 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19590107/): multilayer irregular iris tissue and optical transport are the right direction for the next geometry/optics stage. We have not reconstructed its full inverse procedure or volumetric scattering.
- [Unity digital-human](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human): a mature editable Unity teacher with eye shaders, marker-driven occlusion and cross-material tearline normal blending. Its code is [Unity Companion License](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human/blob/master/LICENSE.md), dependent on permitted Unity use; [the current license](https://unity.com/legal/licenses/unity-companion-license) is not a general cross-engine port grant. Shader/runtime paths were identified but no code was copied into this browser implementation. The separate [sample license](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human.sample/blob/master/LICENSE.md) limits its assets to educational/noncommercial uses. No sample head, eye mesh or image is imported.
- [Epic official Photorealistic Character](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/photorealistic-character?application_version=4.27): teaches tight coupling between eye UVs, geometry and shader, with sclera/iris/limbus/pupil/cornea controls and shader-based refraction. This is an architecture comparison, not a new UE website or a port of engine/sample source. No Epic assets or code are included.
- [HumanShaders eye_shader.gdshader, pinned 95a54cff4a9165993914143ca9047a5f5d029bce](https://github.com/matmadness/HumanShaders/blob/95a54cff4a9165993914143ca9047a5f5d029bce/HumanShaders/eye_shader.gdshader): inspected as an actually editable MIT shader teacher. It separates diffuse iris normals from the wet specular normal and uses a view/parallax approximation; it is not physical multi-interface eye transport. The repository explicitly distinguishes shader/resources from the uncertain Digital Emily model license. We do not take its textures or model and do not relabel it as a top-tier measured eye.
- [EyeNeRF, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08428): useful explicit cornea + implicit eye-interior/periocular architecture. A project-site license is not evidence that trained code/data weights are available or reusable. Do not confuse `HadiZayer/eyenerf` (The Radiance Field in an Eye) with this paper's implementation. No NeRF system or neural weights are claimed here.

## Tissue differentiation and dimensional honesty

[Larsson et al., 2011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3155193/) separately studies crypts, contraction furrows and pigmented rings. This supports distinct controls rather than recoloring one texture. Our three invented presets vary crypt count/depth, radial bundles, collarette relief and peripheral furrows. They are not diagnoses, demographic templates or measured individuals. Photographs in that paper remain copyrighted and are not imported.

[Electron microscopy, 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8602783/) examines pigment content in light/dark irides. Our linear RGB colors and per-channel absorption-like modulation are artistic approximations, not a calibrated melanin concentration, spectral solver or measured collagen scattering coefficient. In particular blue is not represented as a claim about blue pigment.

[Corneal optical properties](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2795748/) gives approximately 7.5–8.0 mm central radius (typical 7.8), nominal corneal IOR 1.376, and central/peripheral thickness differences. These are reference ranges, not a measurement of the KAOPU person. OpticalReference.mjs tests Snell refraction through two concentric spherical boundaries, explicitly a central-zone approximation. It is not yet attached to the native cornea.

[Tear-film interferometry](https://www.ngs.noaa.gov/RESEARCH/RSD/main/products/tearfilm.html) provides a micrometre-scale reference, about 3 µm in limited observations. We must not call a 0.1–0.5 mm mesh clearance a tear film. E1's wet roughness is dimensionless and not equated with tear-film thickness. A separate lower-lid meniscus should follow the user's existing lid curve only after that interface is verified.

EyeParameters.mjs records units, defaults, allowed test ranges, source and uncertainty. Only pupilDiameterMM, irisReliefMM, limbusWidthMM, wetRoughness and preset are active in the native material candidate. The other parameters belong to the isolated optical reference. Current native iris radii are calculated in GNM **bind space** with the same rule as FaceFields. Anisotropic eye deformation and current world-space radius are not inferred to equal those bind-space dimensions.

## What changed and what remains

- Added an ET08 regression-locked baseline and periodic, deterministic polar tissue fields. Height, crypt and pigment are separate channels; presets alter structure. No photo asset is required.
- Added an additive onBeforeCompile bridge, with exact A/B disable and disposal paths. Native geometry, indices, fEye mapping, lids and skin/identity remain untouched.
- Added a separate two-interface CPU optical reference so angle/IOR/radius assumptions can be tested before joining the actual native eye.
- Still missing: measured/aspherical native corneal geometry, full gaze-axis frame extraction, true 3D crypt occlusion, spectral stroma transport, caustics, anatomically fitted sclera vessels and a proper tear meniscus. Refraction is deliberately disabled in the native candidate until the geometry/frame audit passes.
- This is the first narrow comparison experiment, not completion of the requested full eye rebuild. Do not replace the production eye or advertise film quality on the basis of these tests.

## Required visual acceptance

Same ET13 face, same camera, same light: original / dense brown / open hazel / radial blue; front / 30° / 60°; pupil diameter sweep; relief on/off; wet roughness sweep. Inspect actual SwiftShader PNGs, not generated illustrations. Check native vertex/index identity and original fEye data, eye-white exposure, collarette near the pupil, crypts under directional light, no one-pixel streaking, and an A/B-off image that exactly restores original pixels. CI only uploads evidence and has contents:read; it does not commit builds, merge or deploy.
