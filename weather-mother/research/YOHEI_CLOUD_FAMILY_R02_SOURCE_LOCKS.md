## R0.2 production interpretation

The first reviewable family will cover eight archetypes with materially different structure rather than eight color presets:

1. Cumulus humilis
2. Cumulus congestus
3. Stratocumulus
4. Stratus
5. Altocumulus
6. Altostratus
7. Cirrus
8. Cumulonimbus incus

Each archetype must have its own envelope, height/thickness metadata, density remap, erosion distribution, temporal phase speed and optical balance. Shared Yohei-derived correlated fields may organize detail, but they may not erase type identity.
