## Current user direction takes precedence over older production suggestions

The user asks to avoid restricted sources whenever possible, explain restrictions explicitly, and study real-world structure/movement from independently observed or appropriately licensed sources rather than being trapped by a particular author asset. Converting protected source expression into numbers, fitted functions, residuals or another filename is not a rights reset. Do not describe deliberate recreation of an author's distinctive protected output as independent merely because there is no source mesh in the runtime.

Facts, natural phenomena and general methods can inform new work; the particular photographs, videos, source models, textures, animation choices and software expression are separate. The U.S. Copyright Office states the facts/ideas/methods distinction, but this does not decide every reconstruction or every jurisdiction. Keep evidence of independently obtained measurements and independently authored implementation. This is conservative project admission policy, not a legal adjudication or a guarantee against infringement.

Prefer owned observations and verified CC0/public-domain sources; consider CC BY after checking source authority, exact licence and attribution/change requirements. Avoid new production dependencies on NC, ND, unclear or source-conflicted material. NC does not mean merely 'do not sell the final file': a private development activity is not automatically noncommercial. ND 4.0 differentiates adaptations from technical format modifications; do not infer either blanket prohibition of all technical inspection or blanket permission to distribute a reconstruction.

Keep existing source-derived bass/Manta/turtle/catshark/other restricted coefficients as isolated historical research records, subject to their actual permissions. Do not automatically publish them or use them as commercial product defaults. Shared independently authored mathematical/evaluation code is not automatically restricted simply because it was tested with a source. Content-bearing parameters, residuals, fitted appearance and fitted action dependencies require their own traceability. Retain history; do not silently delete old artefacts or relabel their origin.

## Actual code increment: inherited-source admission check

src/rights-gate.cjs is an additive adapter for declaring a commercial adapted-output candidate's dependencies. It traverses original sources through fitted coefficients, residuals, recolouring and repackaging. Child licence labels and 'self-authored' flags cannot erase declared upstream dependencies. Missing parents, cycles, unknown licences and unresolved source links are held. A permission review is bound to the source's hash and licence; CC BY review additionally needs an attribution and change record. A genuinely independent root has a separately retained creation/independence record bound to its content hash.

The checker operates on declared records. It cannot authenticate a copyright owner's identity, discover deliberately omitted inputs or adjudicate legal exceptions. It does not declare a new universal KAOPU file format. Passing this local candidate check is not visual, production or legal acceptance. It has not yet been wired into every existing exporter or Game Mother; do not claim that the live game is automatically protected by this standalone test.
