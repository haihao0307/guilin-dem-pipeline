# Yellowfin Source Copy — Measurement Gate R001

This gate exists to prevent another generic tuna approximation.

The source-copy build remains locked until measurements are taken from FISH-REF-002 itself. Scientific Yellowfin references are used only to detect and document conflicts; they do not replace the locked source copy target.

## Required measurements before copying geometry

The 3D study workbench must record:

1. eye diameter and eye center relative to head/snout;
2. upper-jaw and lower-jaw endpoint relation;
3. mouth-corner location;
4. visible operculum free edge and overlap relation;
5. root start/end of first dorsal, second dorsal, anal, pectoral and pelvic fins;
6. free-edge length and peak location of second dorsal and anal;
7. dorsal and ventral finlet count, spacing, tilt, root width and height rhythm;
8. minimum peduncle width/depth;
9. main lateral keel and two minor-keel positions;
10. pectoral tip reach relative to the second-dorsal root;
11. visible material/color boundaries.

Only then may `YELLOWFIN-SOURCE-COPY-R001` be created.

No artistic improvement is allowed in Source Copy R001. Its only job is to copy the locked source.
