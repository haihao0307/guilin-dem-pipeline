# Tuna R02 / local R10 lineage — REJECTED_VISUAL_LOW_FIDELITY

Date: 2026-09-20

This production line is frozen as failure evidence and must not be used as the continuation baseline.

## Rejection reason

The line reduced a high-information source fish too early into a generic low-dimensional procedural shell:

- a small set of longitudinal sections with uniform superellipse cross-sections;
- plate-like fin membranes derived from simplified root/free-edge curves;
- generic add-on mouth, lower jaw, eye and operculum parts;
- shader bands and procedural highlights used before the body surface had passed shape comparison;
- software/browser assertions treated as progress despite large fixed-view shape mismatch.

The result could run, but it did not preserve the tuna's continuous head–shoulder–trunk–peduncle line, mouth/jaw/cheek relationships, fin-root integration or caudal architecture. Passing numeric and WebGL checks did not constitute visual or biological acceptance.

## Scope of rejection

Rejected as continuation baseline:

- `tuna-r02` geometry and presentation;
- all local R03–R10 descendants built from the same low-dimensional body/fin representation;
- any reuse of their body sections, plate fins, generic mouth/operculum parts or shader bands as accepted species DNA.

Preserved:
