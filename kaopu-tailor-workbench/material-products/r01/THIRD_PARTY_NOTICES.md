The original KAOPU CommonPerson, native tailor data and rendering code are reused from the user's pinned R0434 workbench. Original assets retain their original licensing restrictions and attribution. This integration does not grant a new blanket licence or commercial certification.

## Knit

Ray/Wave R11 at `haihao0307/Human-Fabric-Workbench`, commit `20b466073bb458408a078260a018460c81f627b9`. The selected original needle curves, whole-stitch colour patterns and complete recipes are evaluated inside the original private repository. Only the requested rendered material fields, source hashes and selected recipes are included here. Private research source is not published by this page.

The centerline follows Keenan Crane's 2023 *A Simple Parametric Model of Plain-Knit Yarns*, https://github.com/keenancrane/plain-knit-yarn (MIT code, CC BY 4.0 note). The appearance export uses original R11 centerline functions with a new periodic three-ply attribute baker. Original ray-wave spectral transport, rear float carrier clocks, loose fibre geometry, cloth/fibre relaxation, fibre silhouette and microscopic transparency are not claimed to be transferred. The published garment surface is an opaque backed PBR approximation.

## Leather

Original KAOPU procedural LeatherKernel and selected R10 catalogue recipes at `haihao0307/Humanoid-Rig-Lab-Next`, commit `af389cc216ddd01e3b1aa66c972f42b2587bc797`. R10 includes the original R02 material kernel used for these four procedural choices. No Adobe assets, scanned leather package or garment proxy is imported. The source kernel's unlit colour, normal, roughness and occlusion fields and finish settings are retained.

## Denim

KAOPU R07 at `haihao0307/Humanoid-Rig-Lab-Next`, commit `fa143743b1b0f3d74ff827664d06ff119ef87063`. The original standalone source exports its pre-existing unlit yarn-colour and normal buffers at 2048 resolution. Only build resolution and export hooks are adapted. No lit swatch screenshot is used. Explicit on-garment fibre geometry, frayed hems, full original optical response and material-specific cloth simulation are not claimed.

## Runtime

Three.js and OrbitControls are used through the unchanged original CommonViewer runtime under the MIT licence retained in the original workbench. Original source notices and original teacher-asset restrictions remain available with the unchanged native common-person workbench linked from the page.

All garments retain original solver coordinates, index order and static-test status. Material replacement is a visual operation only. It does not recalibrate bending, stretching, friction, thickness or outfit collision, and is not a production pattern/cost/sizing certificate.
