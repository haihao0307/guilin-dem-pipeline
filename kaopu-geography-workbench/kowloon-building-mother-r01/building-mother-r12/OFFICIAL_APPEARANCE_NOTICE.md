# Official appearance textures, not raw camera photographs

These five unmodified JPEG source atlases were extracted on 2026-10-23 from the Hong Kong Lands Department 3D Visualisation Map (Individualised Models), using the public ZIP links in the official map-tile index. See `official-appearance-provenance.json` for the published image paths, byte counts and SHA-256. Exact source-model IDs and UV bindings are recorded in `official-face-textures.mjs`.

Photography capture dates are unknown. Dataset/tile revision dates are not photography dates. KCC's atlas depicts construction/scaffold-wrap appearance. Preserve source UVs/material bindings and independently prove geometric correspondence before transferring image regions to a different model.

The sidebar displays only the texture triangles bound to a verified original source plane, with UV-mask clipping and orthogonal rotation. No building geometry or source pixels are repainted. Lowrise source patches p172 and p173 failed area-registration validation and are excluded; uniform or tiny atlas-fill regions are also excluded. Photographic texture quality and capture state are not upgraded by geometric registration.
