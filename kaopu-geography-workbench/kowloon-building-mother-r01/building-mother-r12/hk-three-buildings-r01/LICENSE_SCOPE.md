# Three-building experiment: sources and limits

Building geometry data: Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Government, Lands Department, Common Spatial Data Infrastructure (CSDI) Portal. The Government and relevant organisations retain intellectual property rights. Data are available under the [CSDI Terms and Conditions](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC); this is not CC0 or a transfer of ownership.

Source dataset: [3D Visualisation Map, non-textured models](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/geoportal/?datasetId=landsd_rcd_1742809441342_98380&lang=en). The experiment uses three existing downloaded models, not a fresh full-territory download. Source glTF and binary buffers are preserved for comparison, with their hashes and node transforms recorded. The current geometry is not proof of historical appearance or unchanged building fabric.

The CSDI terms allow free reproduction and distribution with clear source and rights attribution and other stated obligations. Data are supplied as available, without a warranty of accuracy. [CSDI attribution guidance](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/info/FAQ) was checked on 2026-10-14. This experimental page is not a government-endorsed reconstruction.

KAOPU material leaves are the existing authored brick, plaster and concrete functions frozen at commit `1f46ba78170752a07086caa3e66c87f2fa4c7fcf`. Source hashes, translations and receiver changes must remain distinguished. Reusing a material function does not establish that the real building used that material. New host coordinates, facade bindings, lighting and optional window pattern are experimental choices. Window patterns, moisture, wear and grains are not surveyed facade evidence.

Three.js and OrbitControls retain their included MIT licence and notices. Other included dependency notices apply to their own files. No private reference photos, restricted teacher shaders, source material images, scans or third-party textures are authorised by this document.

The retained STONE_STUDY kind-0 surface credits the selected stone-block profile to LuthBird1, Stone Brick, CC BY 4.0. See `viewer/provenance/STONE_NOTICE.md` for the source link and scope. No source scan, GLB, photo or image texture is distributed.

Measured-boundary recipes preserve source geometry within the reported numerical tolerance. They are a research encoding and editable reconstruction, not a claim to an established universal KAOPU grammar, analytic signed-distance field, or generally superior compression. Source, recipe, shared code and renderer byte costs are reported separately.
