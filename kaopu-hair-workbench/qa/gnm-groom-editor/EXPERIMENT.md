# R9 human grooming editor, accepted R8 appearance retained

The default presentation is the accepted R8 layered-opacity appearance whenever the existing framebuffer/blend capability probe passes. Native stochastic shadows are an explicit comparison and capability fallback. The old R8 URL remains unchanged.

## Controls

- Six presets: original teacher long hair, two complete-source-segment trims, and the three established R6 real-scalp styles (side sweep, swept back, short crop)
- Independent hair color, length, density, physical width, roughness; short-style volume, hairline and front/side/back coverage
- Teacher-authored brows with independent color, source-prefix length, density and physical width
- Existing real-skin upper-lip and chin beard: visibility, four presets, color, density, length, width and regional coverage
- First-screen manual left/right, mouse/touch rotation, automatic rotation with pause, zoom and camera reset
- Exact full reset to original R8 head/curves, colors, lights and layered appearance

The R8 FiberMaterial.js, HairOpacityShadows.js, TeacherGroomBinding.js, head model, teacher data and lamps are unchanged. Beard appearance uses the same BRDF with the established shorter-facial-fiber roughness/specular values. It is hidden by default.

## Provenance and validation boundaries

Teacher guide data are derived from Daniel Bystedt's official Blender Hair Styles and retain their original CC BY-SA attribution and license. The two cuts are exact prefix subsets of existing ribbon triangles. They are not newly authored salon grooms. No large teacher reference video, high-resolution image, or restricted head asset was added.

The source-index editor passes full seed-724 numeric tests at original counts across neutral, smile, surprise, identity and neutral restoration. Source vertices, root bindings, part islands, random data and material inputs remain exact. Width changes are outside the subset-only radius guarantee.

Existing R8 finite-radius root failures remain 3/2/3/3. Full regression, contact acceptance and production readiness remain false. New focused editor tests and screenshots do not erase them. GPU performance is not inferred from index-edit CPU timing. Prior full-frame readPixels timings on SwiftShader were 7.7–9.7 seconds, not mobile real-time performance. No physical iPhone/Safari performance claim.
